I'm Siddharth Jain. I work on software and cloud infrastructure, and I write here mostly to
document things while they're still fresh: outages I helped debug, tools that earned a permanent
spot in my workflow, and side projects that were more interesting than they had any right to be.

Most posts land in one of a few buckets:

- **Incidents and postmortems** — what broke, what I tried, and what actually fixed it
- **Infrastructure notes** — OpenSearch, AWS, and the sharp edges you only find in production
- **Home lab** — Raspberry Pi builds, self-hosted media, networking with Tailscale
- **Workflow** — terminal setup and the commands I reach for daily

If something here saved you an afternoon, that's the whole point. Say hi on
[GitHub](https://github.com/tellsiddh) or [Twitter](https://twitter.com/tellsiddh).
